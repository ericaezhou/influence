how it works

Learn it. Spar it. See what to fix.

  1. 1

    Learn the framework

    Short, self-paced lessons built on real situations, from asking your manager for a raise to Bruce Allyn's talks with the KGB. You'll learn to understand the other side, know your alternatives and bargain with intent.

    NEGOTIATION · LESSON 2

    Know your alternatives

    The other side can feel it when you have nowhere else to go. Before you ask, know what you'll do if they say no.

    3 of 5 cards
  2. 2

    Spar with a real person

    You each get a private brief, then negotiate, persuade or debate live over voice or video. You'll match with someone on the same module, or bring a friend who has the brief. Spar online or in person.

    No one around? Warm up against AI, like playing a bot in online chess.

    YOUR BRIEF

    THEIR BRIEF

  3. 3

    Get feedback from both sides

    During the spar, quick questions capture how each of you is coming across. Afterward, your partner tells you what landed, and our AI evaluator scores you against a negotiation rubric, with talk time, interruptions and pacing alongside.

Questions

Who will I be sparring with?

Another person working through the same module, or a friend you invite who has the brief. You'll each have your own private brief and your own goal.

Do I have to show my face?

No. You can spar with your camera on, by voice alone, or with your video and voice masked. It's your choice.

What if nobody is available?

You can warm up against an AI opponent, like playing a bot in online chess. It's good for reps, but the real thing is the person.

What happens to my sessions?

Spars happen inside the app. To give you feedback, sessions are transcribed and scored by our AI evaluator. Before your first session, you'll see how your data is handled and choose what you're comfortable with. Joining the waitlist records nothing but what you type here.

Is it really free?

Sparring with real people is free, and so are most of the lessons. Pro is for custom scenarios and more AI time. We'll confirm the details before launch.

When can I start?

We're building our first negotiation unit now and will open it to a small first cohort. Waitlist members hear from us first.

Is this therapy or coaching?

No. Influence is a place to practice. If you're dealing with something clinical or unsafe, please talk to a professional.
